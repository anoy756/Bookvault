Use BookVault as a Desktop App (Pin to Taskbar)

Run BookVault in its own window with a taskbar icon and offline storage

Step 1: Download the Standalone App File

Save a permanent copy of BookVault.html to your computer (for example, in your Documents or Desktop folder), then double-click it to open it in Chrome, Edge, or Brave.

Step 2 (Microsoft Edge): Install Page as App & Pin to Taskbar
  1. Open BookVault.html in Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the three dots menu (⋯) in the top-right corner → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  3. Name it BookVault, click Install, and check Pin to taskbar. It will now launch in its own clean window without browser tabs!
Step 2 (Google Chrome / Brave): Create Window Shortcut & Pin
  1. Open BookVault.html in Google Chrome.
  2. Click the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner → Save and share (or More tools) → Create shortcut…
  3. Check the box “Open as window” and click Create.
  4. Launch BookVault from your Desktop, right-click its icon on your taskbar, and select Pin to taskbar.
